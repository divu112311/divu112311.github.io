Hi, I'm Divya. I turn frontier AI into products that matter.

I'm an AI, product and technology leader, advisor and speaker in San Francisco. I advise startups and organizations on AI-driven consumer products and digital innovation.

I have more than a decade in product and software engineering across retail, financial services and telecom, building user-friendly data products from scratch and bringing AI and ML into companies for real business impact. I've led AI and product initiatives at Peet's Coffee, Hexaware and British Telecom.

Most recently I built the first version of Corduroy AI and took it to two pilot customers. I mentor founders at swissnex, teach AI product at Product Gym, and speak on stages like Plug and Play.

My mission is to make AI-powered experiences accessible and actionable for founders, teams and consumers, and to help the next generation of founders in the San Francisco startup ecosystem. Right now I'm deep in agentic AI, building with the newest models and agent frameworks to see what's ready for real products.
