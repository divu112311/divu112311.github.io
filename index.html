Corduroy AI

Building an AI-native trade compliance platform

Trade compliance is a domain where a seemingly simple product classification decision can require interpreting complex product attributes, regulatory rules and authoritative reference data.

Corduroy AI was built around this challenge.

Product approach

Rather than relying on an LLM to generate classification codes, we designed a system that grounded decisions in retrieved classification and regulatory data.

The product brought together:

  • RAG: Retrieved relevant classification references to ground model outputs.
  • Attribute extraction: Identified product characteristics needed to make classification decisions.
  • Agent workflows: Coordinated specialised tasks across the classification process.
  • Tools: Enabled retrieval, structured data processing and validation.
  • Grounded responses: Connected classification outputs to supporting references.

Technical architecture

Python, OpenAI APIs, Pinecone, Supabase, React/Next.js and GCP.

Evaluation

We evaluated the system against verified reference classifications, achieving over 90% accuracy in testing. We also assessed attribute extraction quality, tool selection, execution success, workflow completion and the grounding of supporting references.

The product progressed from customer discovery to MVP development and two pilot customers.
